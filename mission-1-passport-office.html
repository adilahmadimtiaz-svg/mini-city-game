Mission 1: First Drive — Passport Office — Guide

The mission, exactly as the game gives it: "Drive along Khanewal Road to the PASSPORT OFFICE — any vehicle, or on foot!" Reward: $100. This guide walks you through where to go and how to finish it cleanly.

Where you are going

Your destination is the Passport Office, along Khanewal Road. The mission panel in the top-left of the screen always shows this mission's name, its description and a live distance counter in metres — if you ever feel lost, that counter plus the minimap in the top-right corner will walk you in.

About this mission

This is the gentlest mission in the game, and it is designed that way: it teaches you the map. You may complete it in any vehicle or even on foot, and the target zone is generous. The real lesson is the road itself — Khanewal Road runs diagonally across the whole town, and almost everything in MINI QPR hangs off it.

How to complete it

Take the sports car parked near where you start (press E to get in) and simply follow Khanewal Road, watching the distance counter in the mission panel shrink. If you walk it instead, hold Shift to run. Either way, stop inside the glowing target area in front of the Passport Office and wait a moment — the mission completes, $100 is added, and Mission 2 begins at once.

General tips

  • Stay out of trouble. Stealing a vehicle in a rush can raise your wanted level; a calm drive is usually the fastest drive.
  • Hurt? Heal first. The QPR Supermarket heals you for $20 — cheap insurance before a long drive.
  • Stuck on a corner? Get out and walk in. Every mission zone in the game accepts you on foot.
