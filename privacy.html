Privacy Policy — Mini City Complete

Effective date: 30 October 2026

Mini City Complete ("the Game") is a free, single-player browser game developed by Adil. This policy explains what information the Game does and does not collect.

Information we collect

The Game does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking in the Game.

Data stored on your device

The Game may save your progress and settings (such as money earned and mission state) only in your own browser's local storage, on your own device. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can erase it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Children's privacy

The Game is a general-audience game and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
