MINI QPR — Frequently Asked Questions

Is MINI QPR free?

Yes. MINI QPR is completely free to play. There is no payment, no subscription and no in-game purchase — you open the page, press Play, and the whole town, all vehicles and all six missions are available to everyone.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No. The game runs directly in your web browser. There is nothing to install and no sign-up form to fill in. As long as the page loads, you can play — on a computer, a laptop, a tablet or a phone.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. MINI QPR is built to run in a phone browser, with an on-screen joystick and touch buttons replacing the keyboard controls. It also works on desktop and laptop browsers with keyboard and mouse. For the full control list, see the How to Play page.

How do I save my progress?

MINI QPR is a session game: your cash, health and mission progress are kept while you are playing in that browser session. There is no account and no cloud save, so if you close or reload the page you start a fresh session in the town. Sessions are quick to get going — you begin with starting cash and the first mission right away.

Why is it called MINI QPR?

QPR is the local short name for Qadirpur Rawan, the real town near Multan, Pakistan, where the game is set. "Mini" is because the game is a miniature version of that town — its diagonal Khanewal Road, its landmarks and its street life, all shrunk into one small open world.

Is MINI QPR available on Android?

Yes. Alongside the browser version, MINI QPR is published as an Android app on APKPure, so you can install it and launch it from an app icon. The browser version on this page always works without any install at all.

Who made MINI QPR?

The game was designed and built by Adil, a student developer from Qadirpur Rawan. He made it as a tribute to his hometown — its roads, landmarks, rickshaws and morning milkman — and it may be the first video game ever set in Qadirpur Rawan.

Is MINI QPR a GTA game?

MINI QPR is a small, fan-made open-world game inspired by the style of games like Grand Theft Auto — drive anywhere, take missions, watch out for the police. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Rockstar Games or the GTA series. Players in Pakistan often find it by searching for "GTA QPR" or "GTA Qadirpur Rawan".

What are the system requirements?

Very light. You need a modern web browser (such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari) and an internet connection to load the page. There is no graphics card requirement and nothing to install — if your device can browse the web, it can almost certainly run MINI QPR.

Does the game have sound?

Yes, MINI QPR has music and sound effects. Press the M key at any time to turn sound on or off. On phones, sound starts once you begin playing, as browsers require a tap before audio can play.
