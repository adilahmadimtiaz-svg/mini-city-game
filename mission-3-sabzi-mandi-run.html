Mission 3: Sabzi Mandi Run — Guide

The mission, exactly as the game gives it: "Pick up fresh vegetables from SABZI MANDI (north-west) — head right into the market stalls!" Reward: $120. This guide walks you through where to go and how to finish it cleanly.

Where you are going

Your destination is the Sabzi Mandi, in the north-west of the map. The mission panel in the top-left of the screen always shows this mission's name, its description and a live distance counter in metres — if you ever feel lost, that counter plus the minimap in the top-right corner will walk you in.

About this mission

The Sabzi Mandi is Qadirpur Rawan's fruit and vegetable market, and in the game it is a tight maze of stalls in the north-west of the map. This mission sends you right into the middle of it — the target zone covers the market itself, so do not stop at the edge.

How to complete it

Drive north-west and slow down as you reach the market: the gaps between stalls are narrow, and a smaller vehicle — a bike or a rickshaw — threads through far more easily than a wide car. Nudge into the centre of the stalls until the mission completes and the $120 is paid. If you get wedged, get out (E) and walk the last few metres; the zone accepts you on foot too.

General tips

  • Stay out of trouble. Stealing a vehicle in a rush can raise your wanted level; a calm drive is usually the fastest drive.
  • Hurt? Heal first. The QPR Supermarket heals you for $20 — cheap insurance before a long drive.
  • Stuck on a corner? Get out and walk in. Every mission zone in the game accepts you on foot.
