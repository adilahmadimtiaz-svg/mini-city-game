About MINI QPR — the Qadirpur Rawan Game

MINI QPR is a free, GTA-style mini open-world game that runs entirely in your web browser. It is set in Qadirpur Rawan, a real town near Multan in Punjab, Pakistan — the town its developer calls home. Instead of a fictional city, the game rebuilds a small, real place: its main road, its hospital, its college, its market and its everyday street life, all packed into one playable mini city you can explore on foot, by car, by rickshaw, or even from the air.

The town: Qadirpur Rawan

Qadirpur Rawan — QPR for short — is a small town on the Khanewal Road side of Multan district. It is the kind of place where everybody knows the main bazaar, the morning starts early, and the vegetable market is the busiest spot of the day. MINI QPR tries to capture exactly that feeling. The town's main road, Khanewal Road, cuts diagonally across the map just like the real road cuts through town, and the mohalla houses, shops and streets are arranged around it.

Real landmarks you can visit

The map is built around places that anyone from Qadirpur Rawan will recognise. You can drive to the Passport Office, park outside Zaheer Hospital and Trauma Center, pass the Govt Degree College, and weave through the Sabzi Mandi, the town's fruit and vegetable market. The Jamia Masjid Muhammadia Salfia stands in the town, the QPR Supermarket is open for in-game shopping, and the streets are lined with the kinds of local shops — cloth houses, garment stores, electronics shops and food spots — that give the real bazaar its character.

Town life, not just streets

A town is its people and its routines, so MINI QPR fills the streets with everyday life. Early in the morning the doodh wala — the milkman — makes his rounds on his bicycle. School children walk to class, neighbours go about their day, and the animals of a Punjabi town are all here too: cows resting by the roadside, street dogs, cats, and birds overhead. It is a living little town, not an empty racing track.

Every vehicle in town — and above it

You can drive cars and motorbikes, ride or steal an auto rickshaw, and take to the sky in a helicopter and an airplane that lifts off from the runway when it gathers speed. Stealing vehicles and starting fights will raise your police wanted level, so the town's law is never far behind you. Six missions at real landmarks, an earnable cash system and a working supermarket tie the free-roam world together.

Made by Adil, in Qadirpur Rawan

MINI QPR was designed and built by Adil, a student developer from Qadirpur Rawan who studies and works in digital marketing alongside his studies. The game is a love letter to his hometown — possibly the first video game ever made about Qadirpur Rawan. It is completely free, needs no download or installation, and runs in the browser on a phone or a computer. It is also available as an Android app on APKPure for players who prefer an app icon on their home screen.
