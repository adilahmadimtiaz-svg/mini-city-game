About the developer

MINI QPR was created by Adil, a student developer from Qadirpur Rawan, near Multan in Punjab, Pakistan. Alongside his studies, he works in digital marketing and builds projects for the web. MINI QPR is his tribute to his hometown: a free, browser-based open-world game that recreates Qadirpur Rawan — its diagonal Khanewal Road, the Passport Office, Zaheer Hospital, the Sabzi Mandi market, its rickshaws and its morning milkman — so that anyone, anywhere, can visit the town and play in it.

The game is an independent, one-person project. It is published for free, with no accounts and no sign-ups, and it continues to be maintained and improved by its developer. If you are from Qadirpur Rawan or the Multan area and you spot a landmark you recognise — or one you think should be in the game — that kind of feedback is exactly what the project runs on.

How to get in touch

The best ways to reach the developer, send feedback, report a problem with the game, or ask a question are through the game's public pages:

  • itch.io page: adilahmadimtiaz.itch.io/mini-city-complete — you can leave a comment on the game page there.
  • APKPure listing: MINI QPR on APKPure — the Android version of the game, where you can also rate it and read other players' feedback.

Bug reports are especially welcome: if something in the game does not work on your phone or computer, a short comment on the itch.io page saying what device and browser you used helps a great deal. Suggestions for new missions, vehicles or landmarks in Qadirpur Rawan are read the same way.

Before you write

A few quick answers that save you a message: the game is free and always will be on this website, and there is no account to create, so nobody will ever ask you to sign up or pay to play. If the game seems slow, try closing other browser tabs and reloading the page — it runs best in an up-to-date browser such as Chrome. If a control is not doing what you expect, the How to Play page lists every key and touch button, and the FAQ page answers the questions players ask most often. For anything else — feedback, ideas, or just to say salaam from your own town — the itch.io comments are the front door. Thank you for playing, and for visiting QPR.
