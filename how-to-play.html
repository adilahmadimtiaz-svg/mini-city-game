How to Play MINI QPR

MINI QPR starts on the start screen — press the Play button and you are standing in Qadirpur Rawan with some starting cash, a full health bar and the whole town open to you. This guide covers every control in the game, on keyboard and on phone, plus how money, the police wanted level, the supermarket and health work.

Keyboard controls

  • W A S D — Move on foot, drive a vehicle, or fly an aircraft.
  • Shift — Run while on foot.
  • E — Enter a car, motorbike or rickshaw (this is also how you steal one), exit a vehicle, and enter or leave the shop.
  • Space — Climb while flying; handbrake while driving a car.
  • C — Descend while flying.
  • F — Punch and fight on foot. If you have the pistol equipped, F shoots instead.
  • 2 — Equip the pistol (then F fires it).
  • H — Horn, while in a car.
  • M — Turn the game's sound and music on or off.
  • Mouse — Drag with the mouse to look around and move the camera.

Flying the airplane

The airplane sits at the runway. Get in with E, then simply hold W on the runway — as the plane gathers speed it lifts off by itself, no extra key needed. Once airborne, steer left and right with A and D, climb with Space and descend with C. The helicopter uses the same flight keys: Space to climb and C to come back down.

Playing on a phone

On a phone or tablet the game shows touch controls instead of a keyboard: an on-screen joystick on the left to move and drive, and round buttons for actions such as entering vehicles, fighting and flying. Everything in this guide — missions, shopping, earning cash — works the same way on touch.

Earning cash

Cash is the game's money, shown at the top of the screen. The main way to earn it is completing missions: follow the mission marker to a real town landmark, reach the destination, and the reward is added to your balance. Keep an eye on the mission panel so you always know where to head next.

The police wanted level

Qadirpur Rawan has a police force, and it pays attention. Stealing vehicles and starting fights raise your wanted level, shown as stars on screen. The higher the level, the harder the police come after you. If you want a quiet life, drive your own way around town and keep your fists down; if you steal a rickshaw in front of everyone, be ready to run.

Shopping at the supermarket

The QPR Supermarket is a real, enterable shop. Walk in with E and two things are on sale inside: press B to heal yourself for $20, and N to buy pistol ammo for $10. Press E again to leave the shop.

Watching your health

Your health bar sits at the top of the screen. Fights, crashes and police attention all wear it down. The quickest recovery is a trip to the supermarket — healing costs $20 with the B key — so it pays to keep some cash in reserve instead of spending everything on ammo.
