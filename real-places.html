Real Places of Qadirpur Rawan in MINI QPR

MINI QPR is not set in an invented city. Almost every important building in the game is a real place in Qadirpur Rawan, the town near Multan where the game's developer lives. This page is a short tour of the landmarks you can drive to, walk into, or complete missions at — the same places the town's own people pass every day.

Driving down Khanewal Road — the game's main road, with its street sign, traffic lights and the occasional cow, just like the real thing.

Khanewal Road

The spine of the town and of the game. Khanewal Road cuts diagonally across the map, lined with shops, street lights and the town's traffic. Learn this road and you can find everything else: most landmarks sit on it or just off it.

Passport Office

The destination of Mission 1, "First Drive". In a town where many families have someone working abroad, the passport office is a familiar landmark — so it is fitting that the game's first journey ends there.

Zaheer Hospital and Trauma Center

The town's hospital, and the destination of the game's most urgent mission, "Hospital Emergency" (Mission 4). In everyday QPR life this is the place every family knows the way to without thinking.

Govt Degree College and Associate College

The colleges where the town's students study. The Degree College playfield is the drop-off point for Mission 5, "College Drop" — arrive at the open ground in front of the college to complete it.

Sabzi Mandi

The fruit and vegetable market in the north-west of the map, and the busiest corner of the real town's mornings. In the game it is a maze of stalls you drive right into for Mission 3, "Sabzi Mandi Run".

Jamia Masjid Muhammadia Salfia

The town's Jamia Masjid, with its open ground in front — the destination of the final mission, "Jamia Masjid Visit". It stands as one of the most recognisable buildings in QPR.

The bazaar shops

Along the streets you will pass Al Haadi Foods, Dubai Garments & School Uniforms, Nandhla Cloth House, Pizza Click (the destination of Mission 2, in the south-east), Sultan Bahoo Electronics and Mazhar Electronic — the real names of the real shops of the town's bazaar. The QPR Supermarket is the one shop you can go inside: walk in to heal for $20 or buy ammo for $10.

Every one of these places exists. If you are from Qadirpur Rawan, open the game and drive home. If you are not, this is a small Pakistani town exactly as its own people know it.
