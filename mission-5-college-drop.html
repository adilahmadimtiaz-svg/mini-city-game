Mission 5: College Drop — Guide

The mission, exactly as the game gives it: "Drop the students at GOVT DEGREE COLLEGE — the playfield in front of the college." Reward: $130. This guide walks you through where to go and how to finish it cleanly.

Where you are going

Your destination is the Govt Degree College — the playfield in front of it. The mission panel in the top-left of the screen always shows this mission's name, its description and a live distance counter in metres — if you ever feel lost, that counter plus the minimap in the top-right corner will walk you in.

About this mission

Time to take the students to college. The Govt Degree College stands on the western side of the map, with the Associate College nearby, and the drop-off point is the playfield in front of the college — open ground, so this is one of the easiest targets to hit cleanly.

How to complete it

Drive west across town, using Khanewal Road for most of the distance, then turn off towards the college buildings. The target zone sits on the open playfield in front of the college, so you can drive straight onto it without threading through streets. Stop in the zone, collect $130, and the final mission unlocks.

General tips

  • Stay out of trouble. Stealing a vehicle in a rush can raise your wanted level; a calm drive is usually the fastest drive.
  • Hurt? Heal first. The QPR Supermarket heals you for $20 — cheap insurance before a long drive.
  • Stuck on a corner? Get out and walk in. Every mission zone in the game accepts you on foot.
