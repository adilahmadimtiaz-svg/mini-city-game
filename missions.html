Missions in MINI QPR

Free-roaming Qadirpur Rawan is fun on its own, but the town also has a job for you. MINI QPR contains six missions, and every one of them is tied to the real landmarks of the town — the same offices, hospital, college and market streets you can visit in free roam. Missions are the game's main way to earn cash, and they double as a guided tour: by the time you finish them, you will know Qadirpur Rawan like a local.

How missions work

The system is simple and stays the same throughout the game. When a mission is active, the mission panel on screen tells you where you need to go, and a marker points you towards the destination. Travel there however you like — on foot, by car, by motorbike, or by rickshaw — reach the marked landmark, and the mission is complete. Your cash reward is added straight to the balance shown at the top of the screen, and the next mission becomes available. There is no complicated menu to learn: read the panel, follow the marker, arrive, get paid.

Your first mission: Khanewal Road to the Passport Office

The first mission eases you into the town. It takes you from Khanewal Road — the diagonal main road that cuts across the whole map — to the Passport Office, one of QPR's best-known buildings. It is a short, friendly introduction: learn the driving controls, follow the marker down the main road, and pull up at the office to collect your first reward. From there the missions open up and send you further across town, to the other landmarks on the map.

Landmarks as mission locations

Because the missions are anchored to real places, each one feels like an errand in a real town rather than a level in an abstract city. The destinations are drawn from the landmarks that define Qadirpur Rawan — the Passport Office, Zaheer Hospital and Trauma Center, the Govt Degree College, the Sabzi Mandi market and the streets around Khanewal Road. Part of the fun is recognising the route: these are the same journeys people in QPR make every day, turned into game objectives.

Tips for completing missions

  • Take a vehicle. Walking works, but a car, bike or rickshaw gets you across town much faster. Press E next to a vehicle to get in.
  • Watch the mission panel. It always names your current destination — if you feel lost, read it again and follow the marker.
  • Mind the police. Stealing a vehicle in a hurry can raise your wanted level and turn a simple errand into a chase. If the stars are up, drive carefully until things calm down.
  • Keep some cash back. Mission rewards add up, but save a little for healing at the QPR Supermarket ($20) in case a mission goes wrong.
  • Learn the diagonal. Khanewal Road cuts across the map at an angle — once you can read it, every landmark is easy to reach.

Six missions, six slices of real town life. Finish them all and you will have seen Qadirpur Rawan the way its own people see it — from the inside.
