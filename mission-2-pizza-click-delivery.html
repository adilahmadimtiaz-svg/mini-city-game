Mission 2: Pizza Click Delivery — Guide

The mission, exactly as the game gives it: "Reach PIZZA CLICK (south-east) — a hot pizza is waiting for you!" Reward: $80. This guide walks you through where to go and how to finish it cleanly.

Where you are going

Your destination is Pizza Click, in the south-east of the map. The mission panel in the top-left of the screen always shows this mission's name, its description and a live distance counter in metres — if you ever feel lost, that counter plus the minimap in the top-right corner will walk you in.

About this mission

A short, simple delivery run to the town's pizza shop. Pizza Click sits in the south-east of the map, among the bazaar shops, so this mission is really about learning the south-eastern streets while the pizza is still hot.

How to complete it

From the Passport Office, head south-east across town — the distance counter in the mission panel is your guide, and the minimap keeps you oriented. Park or stop in the target zone by Pizza Click and the $80 reward is yours. Watch for traffic on Khanewal Road as you cross it; a rickshaw is a perfectly good delivery vehicle if the sports car is far away.

General tips

  • Stay out of trouble. Stealing a vehicle in a rush can raise your wanted level; a calm drive is usually the fastest drive.
  • Hurt? Heal first. The QPR Supermarket heals you for $20 — cheap insurance before a long drive.
  • Stuck on a corner? Get out and walk in. Every mission zone in the game accepts you on foot.
