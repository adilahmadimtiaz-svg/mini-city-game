Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This page explains, in plain language, what information is and is not collected when you visit the MINI QPR website and play the game. MINI QPR is a free browser game published by Adil, an independent student developer from Qadirpur Rawan, Pakistan.

The game itself collects no personal data

MINI QPR runs entirely on your own device, inside your web browser. The game has no accounts, no sign-up or login forms, and no chat or messaging features. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or any other personal information, and the game itself does not send your gameplay data to any server. Your progress exists only in your current browser session.

Analytics: Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics (measurement ID G-VK2NE51D5W) to understand, in general terms, how many people visit the site and which pages they read. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects anonymous usage information such as pages visited, approximate region, device and browser type. This information is used only to improve the site and the game. You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings, and the game will still work.

Advertising: Google AdSense

This website uses Google AdSense to show advertisements. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings (adssettings.google.com), and you can learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services at google.com/policies/technologies/partner-sites. Advertising cookies do not give the publisher of this site access to your personal identity.

Third-party links

This site links to third-party websites, including the MINI QPR listing on APKPure and the game's page on itch.io. Those websites have their own privacy policies, and this policy does not cover them. If you follow a link and use another service, please check that service's own policy.

Children

MINI QPR is a general-audience game site. Because the game collects no personal data and has no accounts or communication features, there is no information for any visitor — of any age — to submit to the publisher through the game.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Questions about this policy can be sent through the routes listed on the Contact page.
