MINI QPR Updates and Version History

MINI QPR is a living project. This page records how the game has grown since launch — every major update, in order. The game itself is always the latest version; there is nothing to download or patch, just open the page and play.

The current start screen: PLAY, the Android download, clear buttons to every page of the site, and the town already loaded behind it.

3 November 2026 — Launch

MINI QPR went live on GitHub Pages and itch.io: a free, GTA-style open-world game set in Qadirpur Rawan, with the full town map, drivable cars, bikes and rickshaws, a helicopter and an airplane, six missions at real landmarks, and town life including the morning milkman, school children and animals. The final pre-launch rebuild gave the city and the cars their current look and redesigned the hero into his white suit and black shades.

5 November 2026 — Android app on APKPure

The game was released as an Android app on APKPure, so players can install it on their phones as well as play it in the browser. The same town, the same missions — in an app.

Sound update — full Web Audio

A later update added complete sound to the game using Web Audio: engine and street ambience, effects for missions and actions, and music — with the M key (and the on-screen sound button) to turn it on or off. The game had been silent before this; the town sounds alive now.

23 November 2026 — The website grows up

Six full pages were added around the game: About, How to Play, Missions, FAQ, Privacy Policy and Contact — the story of the game, complete guides, and a proper home for everything players ask.

28 November 2026 — Clearer navigation, more guides

The start screen gained big, clear buttons to every page of the site, so the guides are one tap away on phones. This Updates page, the making-of story, the real-places tour, the tips and tricks guide and a detailed guide for each of the six missions were published the same day.

More updates will be added here as the game grows. If you have an idea for the town, the Contact page is the place to send it.
