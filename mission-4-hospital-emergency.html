Mission 4: Hospital Emergency — Guide

The mission, exactly as the game gives it: "EMERGENCY! Rush the patient to ZAHEER HOSPITAL right away — stop in front of the hospital!" Reward: $150. This guide walks you through where to go and how to finish it cleanly.

Where you are going

Your destination is Zaheer Hospital — stop in front of the building. The mission panel in the top-left of the screen always shows this mission's name, its description and a live distance counter in metres — if you ever feel lost, that counter plus the minimap in the top-right corner will walk you in.

About this mission

The best-paid mission in MINI QPR, and the most urgent in spirit: a patient needs Zaheer Hospital, now. The target is the ground in front of the hospital, next to the Trauma Center — in real Qadirpur Rawan life, this is the drive every family knows by heart.

How to complete it

Take the fastest vehicle you can find and go straight there — this is not the mission for sightseeing. Approach the hospital front and brake inside the target zone; you must actually stop in front of the hospital, not just pass it. Use the handbrake (Space) to pull up sharply at the door. Completing it pays $150, the largest single reward in the game.

General tips

  • Stay out of trouble. Stealing a vehicle in a rush can raise your wanted level; a calm drive is usually the fastest drive.
  • Hurt? Heal first. The QPR Supermarket heals you for $20 — cheap insurance before a long drive.
  • Stuck on a corner? Get out and walk in. Every mission zone in the game accepts you on foot.
