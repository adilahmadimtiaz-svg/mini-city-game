MINI QPR Tips and Tricks

You can finish MINI QPR just by following the mission panel — but the town rewards players who know its tricks. Everything below comes straight from how the game actually works.

Money: missions pay $700 in total

The six missions pay $100, $80, $120, $150, $130 and $120 — that is $700 across the full set, on top of the $250 you start with. The Hospital Emergency (Mission 4) is the best-paid job in town at $150. There is no fuel, rent or repair bill, so mission money is yours to keep or spend at the supermarket.

The QPR Supermarket is your safety net

Walk into the QPR Supermarket and you can heal for $20 (key B inside the shop) and buy ammo for $10 (key N). If a fight or a rough landing has hurt you — a hard fall from the air costs real health — top up at the supermarket before starting the next mission instead of struggling on.

Police and the wanted level

Stealing vehicles and starting fights raises your wanted level, shown as stars in the top panel, and the police will come after you. Two habits keep missions simple: take parked vehicles instead of pulling someone out of theirs, and if the stars do go up, drive calmly and keep out of trouble until things cool down. A clean run to a mission marker is always faster than a chase.

Getting around faster

Press E next to any vehicle to get in — cars, motorbikes and auto rickshaws all work, and the sports car near where you start is the quickest ride in town. Shift makes you run on foot and the handbrake (Space in a car) saves you on tight corners. The minimap in the top-right corner always shows where you are; the mission panel shows how many metres remain to your target, so check the distance instead of guessing.

Flying: the runway trick

The airplane does not need a button to take off. Taxi onto the runway, hold W, and let it build speed — it lifts off by itself. Steer with A and D, climb with Space, descend with C. The helicopter is simpler: Space climbs, C descends. Come down gently: landing too hard costs health.

Small keys, big help

H blows the horn in a car or on a bike — useful for clearing your own path through the bazaar. M turns the sound on and off. Dragging the mouse looks around, which is the fastest way to spot your mission marker in a crowded street. On a phone, everything is on screen: joystick to move, buttons to act.
